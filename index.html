SHARP: A Hybrid Approach for SPARQL Query Relaxation

SEMANTiCS, Vienna

Fakih, G., Serrano-Alvarado, P., Perrin, M. (2025)

Existing SPARQL relaxation methods handle either ontology-based or entity-based elements separately. SHARP proposes a hybrid relaxation model with an information-content-based ranking system, extending similarity measures to entities and literals, and introduces a human-judgment-based evaluation benchmark.

  • Nominated — Best Student Paper, R&I track

2024

A Survey on SPARQL Query Relaxation under the Lens of RDF Reification

Semantic Web Journal, 15(6), 2507–2554

Fakih, G., Serrano-Alvarado, P. (2024)

A survey of SPARQL query relaxation approaches, focused on the challenges posed by RDF reification. Reviews techniques across standard reification, named graphs, n-ary relations, singleton properties, and RDF-star, covering both ontology-based relaxation and instance-similarity-based methods.

2023

Evaluating Reification with Multi-valued Properties in a Knowledge Graph of Licensed Educational Resources

SEMANTiCS, Leipzig

Kieffer, M., Fakih, G., Serrano-Alvarado, P. (2023)

Built a knowledge graph of educational resources where RDF reification captures subject relevance, then experimentally compared four reification models (standard, singleton properties, named graphs, RDF-star) across four triplestores — Virtuoso, Jena, Oxigraph, and GraphDB — over seven SPARQL queries.
